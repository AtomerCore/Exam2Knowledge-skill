# Bloom's Taxonomy Reference for Question Analysis

When analyzing the difficulty and cognitive requirements of an academic question, use Bloom's Taxonomy to classify the core tasks required by the student.

## Cognitive Levels (from lowest to highest)

1. **Remember (Knowledge)**
   - **Definition:** Recalling facts, terms, basic concepts, and answers without necessarily understanding what they mean.
   - **Keywords:** define, duplicate, list, memorize, recall, repeat, state.
   - **Question Types:** Multiple choice (factual), fill-in-the-blank (definitions).

2. **Understand (Comprehension)**
   - **Definition:** Demonstrating an understanding of facts and ideas by organizing, comparing, translating, interpreting, giving descriptions, and stating main ideas.
   - **Keywords:** classify, describe, discuss, explain, identify, locate, recognize, report, select, translate.
   - **Question Types:** Short answer (explanations), concept matching.

3. **Apply (Application)**
   - **Definition:** Using acquired knowledge to solve problems in new situations by applying acquired knowledge, facts, techniques, and rules.
   - **Keywords:** choose, demonstrate, dramatize, employ, illustrate, interpret, operate, schedule, sketch, solve, use, write.
   - **Question Types:** Standard calculation problems, physics free-body diagrams, basic coding tasks.

4. **Analyze (Analysis)**
   - **Definition:** Examining and breaking information into parts, determining how the parts relate to one another, identifying motives or causes, making inferences, and finding evidence to support generalizations.
   - **Keywords:** appraise, compare, contrast, criticize, differentiate, discriminate, distinguish, examine, experiment, question, test.
   - **Question Types:** Data interpretation, reading comprehension (author's intent), complex multi-step math/physics proofs.

5. **Evaluate (Evaluation)**
   - **Definition:** Presenting and defending opinions by making judgments about information, validating ideas, or quality of work based on a set of criteria.
   - **Keywords:** appraise, argue, defend, judge, select, support, value, critique, weigh.
   - **Question Types:** Essay questions, "critique the following argument", peer reviews.

6. **Create (Synthesis)**
   - **Definition:** Compiling information together in a different way by combining elements in a new pattern or proposing alternative solutions.
   - **Keywords:** assemble, construct, create, design, develop, formulate, write, invent.
   - **Question Types:** Research projects, architectural design, writing an original program or essay.

## How to use this in Exam2Knowledge
When evaluating a question, identify highest cognitive level required to reach the solution. Document this in the `📚 Basic Information` section of your output.
