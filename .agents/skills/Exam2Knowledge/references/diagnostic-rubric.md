# Error Diagnostic Rubric

Use this rubric when analyzing the "Common Mistakes and Traps" (Section 4) of a question. Instead of generic advice, precisely categorize the root cause of potential student mistakes.

## Error Categories

1. **Conceptual Errors**
   - **Description:** The student fundamentally misunderstands a core concept, theorem, or formula.
   - *Example:* Believing velocity is zero when acceleration is zero.
   - *Fix strategy:* Revisit the foundational definition; unlearn the specific misconception.

2. **Procedural/Methodological Errors**
   - **Description:** The student uses the wrong path, applies a formula that is not valid under the given conditions, or misses a crucial step.
   - *Example:* Using the quadratic formula when the equation is not in standard quadratic form.
   - *Fix strategy:* Emphasize the preconditions necessary for applying a specific method.

3. **Execution/Calculation Errors**
   - **Description:** Careless mistakes in arithmetic, algebra, sign errors (+/-), or miscopying numbers.
   - *Example:* Writing 2 + 3 = 6, or dropping a negative sign halfway through a calculation.
   - *Fix strategy:* Recommend mental sanity checks, writing larger/clearer, or reverse-checking the final answer.

4. **Reading/Interpretation Errors**
   - **Description:** The student failed to notice a key constraint, assumed information not present, or misread specific phrasing (e.g., "NOT true" vs "True").
   - *Example:* Calculating the radius when the question asked for the diameter.
   - *Fix strategy:* Recommend underlining/highlighting key nouns, numbers, and constraints during the exam.

5. **Edge Case/Boundary Errors**
   - **Description:** Failing to consider limits, divided-by-zero scenarios, domain constraints, or physical impossibilities.
   - *Example:* Forgetting to check if a mathematical solution results in a negative value inside a square root or logarithm.
   - *Fix strategy:* Teach a standard end-of-problem "constraint verification" checklist.
